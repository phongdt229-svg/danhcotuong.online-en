Points System Guide

Learn how to manage your points and participate in competitive games

💰 How to Buy Points

Points are required to play in competitive games where you can win prizes. Here's how to purchase points:

Step-by-step Instructions

  • Sign in to your account

    Make sure you're logged in to access the buy points feature.

  • Navigate to Buy Points

    Go to Points menu → Buy Points, or click here.

  • Choose a package

    Select from available point packages. Each package has a different price and point amount.

  • Complete payment

    Pay using PayPal. Your points will be credited immediately after payment is confirmed.

  • Start playing

    Your points are now in your balance. You can use them to join competitive games.

ℹ️ Payment Methods: We accept PayPal for all transactions. Your payment information is secure and encrypted.

🏦 How to Withdraw Points

If you've earned points by winning games, you can withdraw them and receive money to your PayPal account.

Step-by-step Instructions

  • Sign in and check your balance

    Only points earned through winning are eligible for withdrawal. Your withdrawable balance is shown on the Withdraw page.

  • Go to Withdraw Points

    Go to Points menu → Withdraw Points, or click here.

  • Enter withdrawal amount

    Specify how many points you want to withdraw. You can use the "Max" button to withdraw all available points.

  • Provide PayPal email

    Enter the PayPal email address where you want to receive the money. Double-check this carefully — money sent to the wrong address cannot be recovered.

  • Submit request

    Review everything and click "Request withdrawal". Your points will be held immediately.

  • Wait for approval

    Withdrawals are reviewed manually. Approved requests are processed within 3-5 business days.

Withdrawal Limits & Rules

  • Minimum withdrawal: Usually 100 points or more (check the actual minimum on the withdrawal page)
  • Processing time: 3-5 business days after approval
  • Eligible points: Only points earned from winning games, not purchased points
  • Status tracking: Monitor your request status in the "Your requests" table
⚠️ Important: Withdrawals are reviewed manually and paid by hand. They are not instant. If your request is rejected or cancelled, your points are returned to your balance immediately.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I withdraw points I purchased?

No, you can only withdraw points that you've earned by winning games. Purchased points can only be used to play competitive games.

How long does withdrawal approval take?

Withdrawals are reviewed manually. Most approvals happen within 1-2 business days, and the money is sent within 3-5 days after that.

What if I entered the wrong PayPal email?

Contact support immediately if you notice a mistake before your withdrawal is processed. Once money is sent to the wrong address, it cannot be recovered by us.

Are my payments safe?

Yes, all payments go through PayPal's secure system. We never store your credit card information on our servers.

Can I cancel a withdrawal request?

Yes, you can cancel a pending withdrawal request. Your points will be returned to your balance immediately. Once a withdrawal is marked "Paid", it cannot be cancelled.

Why is my withdrawal status still pending?

Our team manually reviews all withdrawal requests to prevent fraud. If it's been more than 2 days, please contact support.

What's the minimum amount I can withdraw?

The minimum is usually 100 points, but check the withdrawal page for the exact current minimum.

How are the exchange rates calculated?

Check the "Points History" page or the "Buy Points" page to see current conversion rates between points and currency.
