Buying:     $1.00 = 10 points
Withdrawing: 10 points = $1.00  (no withdrawal fee)

We charge one fee only: 20% of the pot of each staked game that has a winner. It is taken automatically at the end of that game, so the points left in your balance are already net of fees. Buying and withdrawing carry no fee from us — see Game payouts & the 20% fee for the full details.

PayPal may charge its own transfer fee when you receive a withdrawal; that fee is set by PayPal, not by us.

🧮 Game Payouts and the 20% System Fee

Every game against another player is played for points. Here is exactly how the points move.

How a staked game works

  • Both players put in the same stake

    When the game starts, the stake is taken from each player's balance. The two stakes together are the pot.

  • The winner receives 80% of the pot

    The remaining 20% of the pot is the system fee, which keeps the service running.

  • A draw costs nothing

    If both players agree to a draw, each gets their full stake back and no fee is taken.

Pot         = stake × 2
Winner gets = pot × 80%  (rounded down to a whole point)
System fee  = pot − winner's share  (20% of the pot)
Winner's profit = winner's share − own stake

ℹ️ In real money: With a 500-point stake ($50), a win returns 800 points ($80) — a profit of $30. A loss costs your $50 stake. The 20% fee on a 1,000-point pot is 200 points ($20), which is the same as 40% of one player's stake.
⚠️ Know the math before you play: Because of the fee, a win earns less than a loss costs (+60% of your stake versus −100%). To break even over time you need to win about 5 out of every 8 staked games. Only stake points you can afford to lose.

When exactly is the 20% fee taken?

  • Once per game, automatically. The fee is taken at the moment a game ends with a winner. You do not pay it by hand and it is never charged twice.
  • From the pot, not from your balance. Nothing extra is taken from your balance on top of your stake. The winner simply receives 80% of the pot instead of 100%.
  • The loser pays nothing extra. If you lose, you lose your stake — no more.
  • No winner, no fee. A draw, or a game that could not start, costs no fee at all. Every point of your stake comes back.
  • Not charged again when you withdraw. Because the fee is already taken at the end of each game, withdrawing converts your whole balance at 10 points = $1 with no further deduction.

How to check it in Points History

Every game you play appears in Points History as one or two rows:

  • 🎲 Stake placed — your stake leaving your balance when the game starts (for example −500).
  • 🏆 Stake won — the winner's 80% share of the pot arriving when you win (for example +800).
  • ↩️ Stake refunded — your full stake coming back after a draw (for example +500).

The fee does not appear as its own row. To see what you paid on a win, use: fee = stake × 2 − Stake won. With −500 and +800, the fee was 1,000 − 800 = 200 points ($20).

How a game ends

  • Checkmate or no legal moves: the server checks every move against the rules and decides the result. Neither player can report a result.
  • Resigning: counts as a loss.
  • Leaving or losing connection: if you are gone for more than 90 seconds during a game, you lose the game and your stake.
  • Draw: only when both players agree. Both stakes are returned in full.
  • Game cannot start: if one player does not have enough points when the game begins, no points are taken from anyone.

🏦 How to Withdraw Points

If you've earned points by winning games, you can withdraw them and receive money to your PayPal account.

    The Withdraw page shows your current balance and the minimum you can withdraw (1,000 points).

  • Go to Withdraw Points

  • Enter withdrawal amount

    Specify how many points you want to withdraw. You can use the "Max" button to withdraw all available points.

  • Provide PayPal email

  • Submit request

    Review everything and click "Request withdrawal". Your points will be held immediately.

  • Wait for approval

Withdrawal Limits & Rules

  • Minimum withdrawal: 1,000 points ($100) per request.
  • No withdrawal fee: the 20% fee was already taken from each game you won, so your whole balance converts at 10 points = $1. The amount shown on the Withdraw page is the amount we send.
  • One request at a time: you can have only one request waiting for review. Wait for it to be paid, or cancel it, before sending another.
  • Points are held immediately: the points leave your balance the moment you submit, so they cannot be staked while the request is being reviewed.
  • The dollar amount is locked in: the USD amount is calculated when you submit and does not change while the request is reviewed.
  • Points for play, not for cash-out: points you bought and never used in a game are meant for playing. We may decline a withdrawal of points that were bought but not played; if we do, the points go back to your balance.
  • Processing time: 3-5 business days after approval.
  • Status tracking: follow your request in the "Your requests" table.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I withdraw points I purchased?

Points are sold for playing. Withdrawals are reviewed by hand, and we may decline a request for points that were bought but never used in a game. If a request is declined, the points are returned to your balance in full.

Is there a fee when I withdraw?

No. The 20% fee is taken from the pot at the end of every game that has a winner, so it has already been paid by the time points reach your balance. When you withdraw, 10 points = $1.00 with nothing deducted by us: 1,000 points send $100.00. PayPal may deduct its own transfer fee from the amount you receive.

Where can I see the 20% fee I paid?

In Points History, each game shows as Stake placed (your stake leaving) and, if you win, Stake won (the winner's share arriving). The fee is the difference: stake × 2 − Stake won. For a 500-point game you see −500 and +800, so the fee was 1,000 − 800 = 200 points.

How long does withdrawal approval take?

Withdrawals are reviewed manually. Most approvals happen within 1-2 business days, and the money is sent within 3-5 days after that.

What if I entered the wrong PayPal email?

Contact support immediately if you notice a mistake before your withdrawal is processed. Once money is sent to the wrong address, it cannot be recovered by us.

Are my payments safe?

Yes, all payments go through PayPal's secure system. We never store your credit card information on our servers.

Can I cancel a withdrawal request?

Yes, you can cancel a pending withdrawal request. Your points will be returned to your balance immediately. Once a withdrawal is marked "Paid", it cannot be cancelled.

Why is my withdrawal status still pending?

Our team manually reviews all withdrawal requests to prevent fraud. If it's been more than 2 days, please contact support.

What's the minimum amount I can withdraw?

1,000 points, which sends $100.00 to your PayPal.

How are the exchange rates calculated?

There is one fixed rate in both directions: 10 points = $1.00, with no fee on buying or withdrawing. The only fee is 20% of the pot of each game with a winner. See Points and Real Money for the full table.

What happens to my stake if my internet drops?

You have 90 seconds to reconnect. If you are gone for longer than that, the game counts as a loss and your opponent receives the payout.
